Retail Premium

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Retail Premium ("the extension") is a Chrome extension for pharmacies that use Script Sense. It records the sales taken on each computer's till and helps staff cash up that till at the end of the day. It is provided by Kieran Erasmuson ("we", "us"). It works alongside Script Sense but is not a Script Sense product. This policy explains what the extension reads, what it keeps, and where.

The short version

  • The extension runs entirely in your browser and talks only to Script Sense.
  • We operate no servers. Nothing the extension handles is sent to us, and there are no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It only reads from Script Sense. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Script Sense.
  • It does not read or keep patient or customer details.

What it reads

With Script Sense open and signed in on the computer, the extension uses your existing Script Sense sign-in and reads, for the pharmacy you are signed in to:

  • payments, refunds and account charges as Script Sense records them when they are taken on this computer: amount, time, payment method, card type, status, the bill or account it applies to, and the staff member who took it;
  • bills finalised or put on account on this computer: bill reference, total, unpaid amount, account reference and any loyalty vouchers applied;
  • at cash-up, the day's payments and account charges from Script Sense, to confirm the figures and to show staff names;
  • this computer's Script Sense hardware settings: the paired EFTPOS terminal, the payment types that open the cash drawer, and the receipt printer name;
  • presses of Script Sense's Open Cash Drawer button, counted as drawer opens with no sale.

The extension asks Script Sense for no patient or customer fields. This is the same access the signed-in user already has in Script Sense.

What is kept, and where

  • In Chrome on that computer only: the till's name, location and settings; a reference list of the payments, account charges and bills taken on the till (as above); petty cash and cash-removed entries with their reasons; each cash-up with its counted amounts, notes and totals; the names of staff who took payments; and the Script Sense sign-in token, which expires about an hour after it was issued.
  • Nothing is kept anywhere else. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it stored. You can export a backup file yourself from the extension's settings; that file stays wherever you save it.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data. The extension sends requests only to Script Sense (scriptsense.co.nz), using your own sign-in.
